About Jesús

I am a citizen of planet earth. At over 39 years old, I have had the pleasure to live in Caracas for 25 years, Barcelona (Spain) for 6 years and now in Sydney.

My journey

  1. 1987 – 2013

    Caracas, Venezuela

    Born here. I graduated as a Software Engineer from Simón Bolívar University (free and high quality education, I have to say) and started working at a small company.

  2. 2013 – 2018

    Barcelona, Spain

    A couple of startups, a master’s degree and the start of a board game habit. I totally blame Pandemic and Catan.

  3. 2019 – now

    Sydney, Australia

    Working at a big company like Atlassian, on the editor behind Jira and Confluence, and starting a new board game collection.

The longer story

I was born in Caracas, where I graduated as a Software Engineer at Simón Bolívar University, and I have been working as a Software Engineer since then: starting at a small company in Venezuela, moving to a couple of startups in Barcelona and finally working at a big company like Atlassian. You can read more on my resume.

I really like staying at home to watch some random TV shows, play my video games or just read a book. It is so relaxing having time with myself (I am getting old, I know). When I am not too lazy, I like to go outside and do some exercise or sport.

Hobbies

Volleyball

I used to play a lot of volleyball back at university and I really liked it. I don’t play as much as I would like, but I am always looking for ways to hit some ball.

Gym, CrossFit & the Tacx

I aim to exercise at least 4 times a week, and CrossFit has been the most successful. It is too expensive in Australia, so I went to a normal gym, and when that doesn’t work my Tacx indoor trainer is my best partner. I am keen to start CrossFit again.

Board games

I got into board games about 10 years ago, thanks to Pandemic and Catan. I left my small collection in Spain and started another one in Australia, where Arkham Horror LCG is the main game.
